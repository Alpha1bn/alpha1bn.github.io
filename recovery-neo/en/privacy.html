Recovery Neo for Android — Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 27, 2026<br> Developer: JIN ATOLL (sole individual developer)<br> Contact / Data protection contact: sungjyny@gmail.com<br> App: Recovery Neo, available on Google Play

This policy explains what Recovery Neo does with data on your device, what little information leaves your phone, and why. It is written to be read, not skimmed, so it is short.

1. The one-sentence version

Recovery Neo scans and recovers files from a USB drive or memory card connected to your phone, entirely on the phone. The files it recovers, and everything it reads from your drive, never leave your device. The only network traffic the app generates comes from Google's advertising, billing and update services, described below.

2. What the app does on your device

  • Reads a USB drive, SD card or microSD card that you connect through an OTG adapter or card reader, in read-only mode. The app never writes to, formats or modifies the connected drive.
  • Analyzes the drive's contents on the phone to find deleted or lost files.
  • Saves the files you choose to recover into the app's private storage and, when you export them, into Downloads/RecoveryNeo on your phone.
  • Keeps recent recovery results on the phone so you can return to them. You can delete them at any time from the app or by deleting the folder in Downloads.

None of this processing uses a server. You can run a complete recovery in Airplane Mode.

3. What never leaves your phone

  • The contents of any drive you connect.
  • The files the app recovers, their names, thumbnails, folder structure and metadata.
  • The index files the app writes to describe a recovery.
  • Any personal files, photos, videos or documents.

The app does not upload, sync, back up, transmit or analyze these on any server, and does not share them with any third party. We have no access to them.

The app does not request access to your photos, contacts, location, camera, microphone or phone internal storage, and does not read files from your phone other than the ones it recovered itself.

6. Data you keep, and how to delete it

Everything the app produces stays on your phone under your control:

  • Recovered files and results: delete them from the app's results screen, or delete the Downloads/RecoveryNeo folder.
  • App settings, language choice, purchase status and recent recovery results: cleared when you uninstall the app or clear its data in Android Settings.
  • Purchase record: held by Google Play under your Google account, not by us.
  • Scan-consent records: the last 50 records of the time you agreed, the Terms version, and the app version. Stored on this phone only; never uploaded.

Because we never receive your data, there is nothing for us to delete on our side and no account to close.

7. Children

Recovery Neo is a general-purpose utility not directed at children under 13. To use the App on your own, you must be at least 13 (or the age of digital consent in your country — for example 14 in the Republic of Korea, 16 in parts of the EU). Below that age you may use the App only with the consent and supervision of a parent or legal guardian. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Ads are served through Google AdMob with content settings appropriate for a general audience.

8. Security

Recovery is performed with the drive opened in read-only mode; the app cannot alter the source drive. Files recovered but not yet unlocked (ATOLL 2 results before purchase) are stored in the app's private sandbox, inaccessible to other apps. We do not operate a server, so no user data is stored on a server.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU/EEA, UK, Brazil, Korea or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict processing of personal data. Since Recovery Neo does not collect personal data on any server, those rights are exercised directly on your device (section 6) and, for advertising and purchase data, through Google:

  • Ad personalization and the Advertising ID: Android Settings → Google → Ads.
  • Ad-consent choices (EU/UK): the consent form shown in the app, which you can reopen from the app's settings.
  • Purchase data: Google Play → Payments & subscriptions.

For any question about this policy, email sungjyny@gmail.com.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app's data practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above, and, for material changes, describe the change in the app's release notes. Continued use after a change means you accept the updated policy.

11. Retention

  • On-device data stays until you delete it or uninstall the app: recovered files (see section 6), app settings, purchase-status cache, language choice, and up to 50 recent scan-consent records.
  • Scan-consent records are capped at the last 50 (time of agreement, Terms version, app version). Older records fall off automatically. They stay on this phone and are never uploaded.
  • Google's copies of the data listed in section 4 — Advertising ID, IP-derived approximate location, app interactions and diagnostics received by AdMob; purchase tokens received by Play Billing; package name + version code received by Play In-App Updates — are held by Google under Google's own retention rules (https://policies.google.com/privacy). We do not receive or store any of that data and cannot request deletion on your behalf; use the Google controls linked in section 9.
  • Our own retention: none. We do not operate a server. There is nothing for us to keep.

12. Data protection contact (Data protection officer / Data protection contact)

Recovery Neo is developed by JIN ATOLL, a sole individual developer. There is no separate legal entity or corporate DPO. For every privacy question — access, deletion, correction, complaint, or regulator inquiry — write to sungjyny@gmail.com. Target first response: within 10 business days. Complaints can also be raised with the data-protection authority where you live (for example, in the Republic of Korea, the Personal Information Protection Commission; in the EU/EEA, your national DPA).

13. Third-party recipients and processors

The only third-party recipient of any data the app generates is Google LLC, through the three SDKs listed in section 4:

  • Google AdMob — Advertising ID, IP-derived approximate location, app interactions, diagnostics (advertising).
  • Google Play Billing — Google account purchase token and product ID (purchase processing / restore).
  • Google Play In-App Updates — app package name and installed version code (update check).

We do not use any other processor, subprocessor, analytics vendor, crash-reporting vendor, tracking pixel, tag manager, or third-party SDK. If that ever changes, this section will be updated before the change ships and the change will be listed in the app's release notes.

14. International data transfer

Recovery Neo does not send data across borders itself — we have no server. The three Google services above are operated by Google LLC on Google's global infrastructure and may process the data in facilities located outside your country of residence. Google's own privacy policy (https://policies.google.com/privacy) explains their cross-border transfer safeguards, including standard contractual clauses where required. By using the App you understand that data received by Google may be transferred to and processed in those regions.

15. Summary for Google Play Data safety

  • Data collected: Device or other IDs — Advertising ID (Google AdMob, advertising, required — collected by the AdMob SDK whenever ads are shown; users outside consent regions cannot opt out without purchasing Lifetime); Location — approximate location derived from IP (Google AdMob, advertising); App activity — app interactions (Google AdMob, advertising); App info and performance — diagnostics and crash logs (Google AdMob, advertising); Financial info — purchase history (Google Play Billing, app functionality).
  • Data shared with third parties: the above, with Google only.
  • Data encrypted in transit: yes (Google services use HTTPS).
  • Data deletion: users can delete on-device data at any time. We do not operate a server, so no user data is stored on a server.
  • The app does not collect files, photos, videos, personal info, contacts, messages, precise location, or the phone's internal storage. Recovered files and drive contents do not leave the device.
